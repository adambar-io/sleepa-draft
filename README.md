# sleepa draft

Mock draft with your real Sleeper league: its teams, its draft order, its traded picks and its keepers, against CPU
drafters that pick like real people.

**Open it:** https://adambar-io.github.io/sleepa-draft/

- Enter your Sleeper username, pick a league, and mock its next draft.
- Keepers: costs come from last season's draft (the round they went in, or the last round for pickups), with
  suggested keepers for every team. Change any keeper or any cost.
- Draft order: set in Sleeper, reverse standings, last year's, random, or your own.
- Rankings: Sleeper ADP for your league's format, FantasyPros expert rankings, or a blend.
- Draft room with a live board, queue, auto-draft, undo, and a recap with projected points and your best values.

A sibling of [sleepa](https://github.com/adambar-io/sleepa). One static page, no server, no tracking, no
third-party scripts. League data is read in your browser from Sleeper's public API. Rankings: Sleeper ADP and
FantasyPros expert consensus (data © FantasyPros, via [DynastyProcess](https://github.com/dynastyprocess/data)).
Not affiliated with or endorsed by Sleeper or FantasyPros.
